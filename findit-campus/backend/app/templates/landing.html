SYSTEM CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Speed, Accuracy & Campus Privacy.

Everything students and staff need to easily manage, locate, and recover lost belongings across campus.

Image Detection

AI vision models classify categories, primary colors, and visual characteristics from uploaded photos.

Smart Text Match

NLP vector embeddings compare descriptions, brands, and item context to calculate precise similarity scores.

Ownership Proof

Multi-question verification ensures items are returned strictly to the true owners without exposing private data.

Campus Hotspot Map

Explore interactive campus heatmaps to see where items are frequently lost or recovered (Library, Canteen, Labs).

Quick QR Kiosks

Scan campus QR codes placed at major college locations to file a found item in under 30 seconds.

Student Leaderboard

Earn points (+10 for reporting found items, +50 for confirmed handovers) and earn campus community badges!
